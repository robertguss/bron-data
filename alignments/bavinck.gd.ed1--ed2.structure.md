# Structural concordance: Gereformeerde Dogmatiek, 1st edition (1895–1901) ↔ 2nd edition (1906–1911)

**Level:** structure (GD¹ § ↔ GD² Par.). **Drafted:** 2026-11-02 by agent from the two tables of contents (GD¹: Gutenberg transcriptions and NRI "1e druk" page; GD²: NRI "2e druk" page, whose titles use modernized spelling). **Status:** `resp="#run-2026-11-02"`, human check pending. Verify GD² titles against the printed 2nd-edition TOC during transcription.

**Totals:** GD¹ 56 §§ → GD² 63 Par. (1–62, with 13 used twice). 56 + 3 insertions + 4 splits = 63. ✓

**Summary of structural changes**
- **Insertions (3):** Par. 9 *Wezen en Begrip van de Openbaring*; Par. 12 *Openbaring en Natuur*; Par. 18 *De religieus-empirische methode*. All in vol. 1.
- **Splits (4):** §11 → Par. 13 + 13b (Scripture → revelation-and-Scripture + inspiration); §26 → Par. 29 + 30 (essential names → incommunicable + communicable attributes); §42 → Par. 46 + 47 (work of the Mediator → humiliation + exaltation); §45 → Par. 50 + 51 (faith-and-justification → faith-and-conversion + justification).
- **Chapter move (1):** §35 *De Voorzienigheid* moves from Hoofdstuk VI (the fallen world, vol. 3) to Hoofdstuk V (the world in its original state, vol. 2) as Par. 39. Providence is relocated from hamartiology to creation.
- **Order swap (1):** §37 *Het wezen der zonde* / §38 *De verbreiding der zonde* become Par. 41 *Verbreiding* / Par. 42 *Wezen en Werking*: spread of sin now precedes its nature.
- **Renames (5):** §25 → Par. 28; §27 → Par. 31 (personal names of God → Holy Trinity); §41 → Par. 45; §50 → Par. 56; Hoofdstuk VII *Over Christus* → *Over de Persoon en het Werk van Christus*.
- **Volume boundary shift:** Hoofdstuk VIII (benefits of the covenant) sits wholly in vol. 3 in GD¹ (§43–46); in GD² it straddles vol. 3 (Par. 48) and vol. 4 (Par. 49–52).
